Private Service Access and Private Google Access

These technologies help workloads stay private by avoiding public exposure while still reaching managed Google services and internal applications.

Home VPC Deep dives

Private Google Access

Private Google Access allows VM instances without external IPs to reach Google APIs and services over private network paths. This is critical for security and for keeping workloads out of public routing.

Private Service Access

Private Service Access creates private connectivity between consumers and Google-managed or internal services, often using allocated private IP ranges. It helps services communicate without exposing them to the public internet.

Design guidance

  • Private Google Access is for reaching Google APIs and services from private VMs.
  • Private Service Access is for private connectivity to managed services or internal endpoints.
  • Use these patterns when security policy requires private-only communication.

What to remember

Memory hook

Private access = no public IP, still private connectivity. If the workload lacks an external IP, use private pathing to Google services or managed internal endpoints.

  • Private Google Access keeps VMs private while reaching Google APIs.
  • Private Service Access supports private reachability to managed or internal services.
  • These patterns matter when business policy requires no public internet exposure.

Typical exam cues

  • “VMs do not have external IPs but must reach Google APIs.”
  • “Workloads need private connectivity to a managed service over RFC1918 ranges.”
  • “The solution must avoid public internet exposure.”
